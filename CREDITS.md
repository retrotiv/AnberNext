# Credits

AnberNext stands entirely on other people's work. It is a personal adaptation and a set
of RG353V fixes — the front-end, the launcher and the platform underneath it were built
by the people and projects below.

## The front-end

- **NextUI** — the firmware this project adapts, by the **LoveRetro** team and its
  contributors. https://github.com/LoveRetro/NextUI · https://nextui.loveretro.games
- **MinUI** — the minimalist launcher NextUI itself is built on, originally by
  **Shaun Inman** (https://github.com/shauninman). The NextUI lineage also runs through
  **ro8inmorgan**'s MinUI work (https://github.com/ro8inmorgan/MinUI).

## Paks and tools from the NextUI ecosystem

The AnberNext image includes, or is designed to work alongside, tools from the NextUI
community. Credit to their authors:

- **NextUI Game Manager** — UncleJunVIP · https://github.com/UncleJunVIP/nextui-game-manager
- **NextUI Updater Pak** — LanderN · https://github.com/LanderN/nextui-updater-pak
  (and the LoveRetro updater pak)
- **NextUI Theme Manager** — Leviathanium · https://github.com/Leviathanium/NextUI-Theme-Manager
- **The NextUI Pak Store** and its contributors, for the on-device app catalog AnberNext's
  own updater is modelled after.

## The RG353V platform

- **dArkOS** — the RG353V distribution whose kernel (5.10.x BSP), Mali driver and heavier
  emulators made the hardware usable under AnberNext.
- **GammaOS** — the stock Android (on the console's internal memory) that AnberNext's `F`
  toggle hands back to, left completely untouched.
- **Anbernic** — for the RG353V hardware and its stock dual-boot mechanism, which the `F`
  toggle mirrors.
